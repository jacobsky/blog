Like any good technology, the benefits of LLMs, coding agents, and all this AI hype really only show up when you are about... six or seven months later. We are officially three years into "Software Engineering is 6 months from being over" and as someone with a frontrow seat to watching the ChatGPT 3.5/4 hype and Chat GPT 5's wet fart, I've always been skeptical. Hell, as someone that was forced into the early adoption, I've been fighting against bad LLM workflows for about 3 years at the time of this writing.
